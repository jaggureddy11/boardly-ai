Why now

Three shifts are converging at once

01
Voice AI just became viable at India-scale cost
India's voice AI market is growing at roughly 35.7% CAGR, projected to go from $153M (2024) to $958M by 2030 — the fastest-growing segment within the broader conversational AI category. Cost per outbound AI call in India has dropped into a range (roughly ₹3–6/min mid-market) that makes proactive, journey-length calling economically plausible for the first time.
02
Government is already building the multilingual rails
The 2025 BHASHINI–CRIS MoU is bringing multilingual speech recognition and TTS into NTES and RailMadad — real infrastructure investment in exactly this direction, but currently scoped as inbound/query tools, not proactive outbound calling. That's the gap this concept sits in.
03
Regulation favors transactional voice AI right now
TRAI's TCCCPR framework draws a real distinction between promotional cold-calling and transactional service calls tied to an existing booking — the latter faces materially fewer restrictions. A proactive travel companion tied to a real PNR sits on the right side of that line by design, not by exception.
The problem

Every journey starts with guesswork

23 million rail passengers and tens of millions of flyers navigate India's transport network every day. Before almost every trip, the same information gap repeats itself.

01
Platforms change without warning
Train platforms and coach positions change at short notice, and passengers usually only find out by checking a board or asking a stranger — leading to last-minute sprints across crowded stations.
02
Passengers misjudge departure timing
Even frequent flyers get flight timing wrong — traffic, security-line length, and gate distance aren't factored into a generic 'arrive 2 hours early' rule, leading to missed flights or wasted waiting time.
03
Existing systems are pull-based, not proactive
IRCTC's Ask Disha is a chatbot passengers have to open and query. NTES shows live status but only if you check it yourself. Airline tools like ASAPP notify you about disruptions, but nothing walks a first-time traveler through the whole journey, start to finish.
"The information passengers need already exists somewhere — NTES, DGCA data, airline systems. Nobody proactively delivers it to the passenger, by voice, in their language, at the moment it matters."

This is the daily experience for a meaningful share of India's 23 million daily rail passengers and 28+ crore annual domestic air travelers.

It's an information-delivery problem that no existing app or chatbot has solved, because all of them wait for the passenger to ask.

How it works

Four steps. Zero missed connections.

Boardly runs automatically from the moment a ticket is booked — no app-checking needed. The same four steps power both journeys below; here's what that looks like for each.

1

Journey linked

Passenger's PNR (train or flight) is linked via booking confirmation — SMS, email forward, or direct IRCTC/airline integration. Boardly picks up journey date, seat/coach, and gate/platform data automatically.

2

AI calls at the right moments

T-24 hours: confirms details and answers document/baggage questions. T-3 to T-4 hours: shares expected platform or a personalized 'leave-by' time factoring live traffic and security-queue length.

3

Listens and understands

'Which platform is it?' → live answer. 'I'm running late' → flagged and rerouted with fastest-path guidance. Real two-way conversation, not a one-way alert.

4

Full journey continuity

One relationship spans booking confirmation through arrival — platform/gate changes, delays, and arrival guidance, all from the same AI, in the passenger's language.

Ground Transit

Train Workflow

Phase 01 Pre-Arrival at Station
  • Proactive Dispatch: Notifies platform number, coach position, and boarding side (engine-end vs. rear) well before arrival.
  • Instant Wayfinding: Resolves spoken natural queries in real time: "Which platform?" / "Where is coach B2?"
Phase 02 Platform & Boarding
  • Dynamic Routing: Guides rushing passengers with real-time walking shortcuts: "Departs in 6 mins from platform 3 — north footbridge is fastest."
  • Zero Congestion: Eliminates passenger dependency on hard-to-hear public PA speakers.
Designed Impact
Fewer missed departures · Drastically reduced platform confusion at scale
Aviation & Air

Flight Workflow

Phase 01 Pre-Departure & Check-in
  • Smart Departure Window: Calls with live traffic & security wait times factored in, plus web check-in reminders and baggage allowances: "Leave by 4:15 PM — 25 min traffic delay on Airport Expressway."
  • Pre-Flight Query Handling: Answers baggage limits, terminal directions, and meal preferences.
Phase 02 Terminal, Gate & Boarding
  • Real-Time Gate Clarity: Instant spoken guidance on gate changes, boarding calls, and connection walks: "Gate changed to 14B — boarding starts in 12 mins."
  • Disruption Recovery: Flight delay or gate change immediately triggers an automated voice call + SMS fallback.
Designed Impact
Fewer missed gates · Calmer, well-informed passenger flow through security

Why this matters for equal ai

Equal AI already proved that Indians will trust an AI layer on their phone — over a million people use it to screen inbound calls. The question this concept explores is narrow and specific: does that same trust extend to AI reaching out to you, for a handful of high-stakes minutes around travel, where getting it right has real consequences?

  • Outbound, not inbound — a genuine extension of Equal AI's voice-AI thesis into a new direction
  • High-frequency, high-stakes use case: 23M+ daily rail passengers, 28+ crore annual domestic air passengers
  • Structured, predictable conversations (platform, gate, timing) — ideal for reliable AI automation
  • Clear, measurable intent detection and outcomes (missed-connection reduction, support-call deflection)
  • Transactional service call, not cold promotional outreach — favorable under TRAI's TCCCPR compliance framework since it's tied to an existing booking
  • Expandable beyond travel into any proactive, time-sensitive consumer-notification vertical
